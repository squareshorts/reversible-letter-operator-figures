# Exact-age redesign update

This file contains aggregate/non-identifying results only. Participant names, DOBs, and the private reconciliation table are not stored in this public repository.

## Demographic reconciliation

The newly supplied 142-row demographic workbook reproduces the later 44/26 Grade-1/Grade-2 descriptive counts, but it is not a clean 142-identity table: it contains duplicate/overwritten identities. Reconciliation against the canonical analysis crosswalk and raw sessions shows that the analytical cohort remains 43/27/32/21/19 across Grades 1-5. The 44/26 shift is generated by the demographic workbook composition rather than by a demonstrated canonical participant recoding.

Exact DOB-to-raw-session chronological age was linkable for 140 of 142 canonical participants. The two unresolved exact ages are both in Grade 2. Among the 140 linked participants, mean age is 8 years 7 months (SD 17.4 months), with a range of 6 years 4 months to 11 years 7 months. The previously reported 5 years 9 months minimum is not supported after canonical identity reconciliation.

## Grade beyond exact chronological age

Nested multinomial likelihood-ratio tests compare categorical grade with the corresponding exact-age model (df=8; n=140, 560 trials per task).

| Task | Linear exact age | Cubic B-spline exact age |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lowercase identification | chi2=10.67, p=.221 | chi2=8.59, p=.378 |
| Mirror judgment | chi2=23.20, p=.003 | chi2=15.66, p=.048; BH=.071 |
| Rotation judgment | chi2=44.50, p=4.57e-7 | chi2=48.91, p=6.61e-8 |

With binary family-income and guardian-education sensitivity adjustment:

| Task | Linear exact age + SES | Cubic B-spline exact age + SES |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lowercase identification | chi2=12.91, p=.115 | chi2=10.42, p=.237 |
| Mirror judgment | chi2=19.40, p=.0129 | chi2=11.28, p=.186 |
| Rotation judgment | chi2=41.75, p=1.51e-6 | chi2=47.57, p=1.20e-7 |

Only rotation is robust across all exact-age and socioeconomic specifications.

## Rotation localization

The existing 2,000-participant cluster-bootstrap categorical-grade contrasts on all 142 participants localize the largest adjacent transition to Grade 3 minus Grade 2:

- left-right reflection: -30.4 percentage points, 95% CI [-44.7, -17.3];
- correct 180-degree rotation: +18.4 points, 95% CI [4.1, 32.8];
- top-bottom reflection: +12.0 points, 95% CI [-1.8, 26.8].

The Grade-4-minus-Grade-3 left-right contrast reverses partly (+14.0 points, 95% CI [2.3, 27.4]), so the pattern is nonlinear rather than a monotonic grade trend.

## Exact-age common support

Grades 2 and 3 overlap in exact age from 8.38 to 9.50 years. Within that interval are 34 participants (6 Grade 2, 28 Grade 3).

Adjusted for exact age and design covariates:
- left-right reflection: -24.0 pp, 95% cluster-robust CI [-45.7, -2.3];
- correct rotation: +14.3 pp, CI [-3.3, 31.8].

With binary SES adjustment:
- left-right reflection: -33.1 pp, CI [-67.1, 0.8];
- correct rotation: +22.8 pp, CI [7.2, 38.3].

Because strict common support contains only six Grade-2 participants, this is a sensitivity analysis rather than the primary inferential basis.

## Repeated-measures sensitivity

A nominal GEE with exchangeable within-participant correlation replaces the earlier false-converged random-intercept sensitivity:

- exact age: Wald chi2(8)=23.44, p=.00285, converged;
- exact age + binary SES: Wald chi2(8)=23.58, p=.00270, converged.

## Two unresolved exact ages

An exhaustive 12 x 12 monthly-age grid assigned every age compatible with the two participants' recorded completed-year ages. In all 144 assignments, the rotation grade-beyond-linear-age + binary-SES result remained strong: LR chi-square 40.76-42.44; largest p=2.44e-6.

Thus the core scientific conclusion does not depend on recovery of the two exact DOBs, although their recovery remains desirable for complete descriptive reporting.

## Manuscript consequence

The paper should be framed around nonlinear grade-associated redistribution in rotation choices. Lowercase identification becomes secondary/null after exact-age adjustment. Mirror judgment is suggestive but not robust to the flexible exact-age + SES specification. The manuscript should not claim a causal effect of schooling, literacy instruction, or maturation.
